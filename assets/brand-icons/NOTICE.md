# Platform logos

Official logos of third-party platforms, used only to name the platform in sales videos (nominative use). They are trademarks of their owners (Meta: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Messenger; Google: YouTube, Gmail, Google Maps, Google; X Corp.; LinkedIn/Microsoft; Telegram). Files downloaded 2026-10-22 from Wikimedia Commons reproductions of the official logos (upload.wikimedia.org); PNGs are 512 px renders of those SVGs.

Rules (from the owners' brand pages where available):
- Never modify: no recolor, no stretch, no rotation, no outlines, shadows or effects on the logo itself (Meta, YouTube). Scaling is allowed.
- Clear space around the logo: at least 1/4 of its width (Facebook, Messenger); YouTube: at least the size of its triangle.
- Minimum size on screen: 20 px (YouTube), 16 px (Messenger); in our videos always ≥ 96 px.
- Never imply the platform endorses the brand. Meta asks for review before use in TV/film; online sales reels are not clearly covered.
- Preferred: replace these with the files from the official kits (download needs accepting terms): meta.com/brand/resources (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Messenger), brand.youtube, TikTok brand guidelines.

TikTok: no verified official file yet — do not draw an approximation; ask the user for the official kit.
